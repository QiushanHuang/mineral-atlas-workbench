# Mineral Atlas Identity

The mark is a faceted green crystal with a gold selected face and a small node-and-edge trace. It connects the workbench's two main views: spatial morphology and face adjacency.

- Forest green: `#183B32`
- Sage green: `#87A895`
- Selected-face gold: `#D3A557`
- Asset: `logo.png`, transparent PNG

Use the supplied asset without distorting its aspect ratio. The icon is a schematic brand mark, not a calibrated mineral model or a diagram from which crystallographic indices should be inferred.
